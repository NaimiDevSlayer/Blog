*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

This blog is a free course website. It has **no user accounts** and asks for no information to read the courses. This page explains the little data that is processed, and why.

## Data controller

The website's publisher, described in the **[legal notice]({{< relref "/mentions-legales" >}})**. For any request: the **[Contact]({{< relref "/contact" >}})** page.

## Audience measurement: Google Analytics

To know which courses are read and improve the website, we use **Google Analytics 4**, a service of Google Ireland Limited.

- Google Analytics is loaded **only if you click "Accept"** in the banner shown on your first visit. If you decline, or don't answer, no data is sent to Google.
- Data collected: pages viewed, visit duration, country, device and browser type, referring site (Google, YouTube…). The IP address is anonymized.
- Cookies set: `_ga` and `_ga_*`, kept for **13 months** at most.
- This data may be transferred to the United States. Google adheres to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

You can change your mind at any time with the **"Cookies"** link at the bottom of every page.

## Preferences stored in your browser

The website stores two small pieces of information in your browser (`localStorage`), which are never sent to anyone:

- your choice in the consent banner (`nds-consent`);
- the light or dark theme you picked (`pref-theme`).

## YouTube videos

The courses embed the channel's videos with YouTube's **privacy-enhanced mode** (`youtube-nocookie.com`): YouTube sets no tracking cookie until you play the video. Once you play it, Google's privacy policy applies.

## Fonts

The website's fonts are loaded from **Google Fonts**. To do so, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. No cookie is set.

## Contact form

If you write to us through the **[Contact]({{< relref "/contact" >}})** page, your name, email and message are sent through the **Web3Forms** service and received by email. They are only used to answer you, and are deleted at the latest **one year** after the last exchange.

## Advertising

The website shows no ads for now. If ads (Google AdSense) are added, this page will be updated and your consent will be asked before any advertising cookie.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to and restrict the processing of your data. To exercise them, use the **[Contact]({{< relref "/contact" >}})** page.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the French data protection authority, the **CNIL** ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).
