Hi, I'm **Naimi**, a software engineer and the creator of the **[NAIMI Dev Slayer](https://youtube.com/@NaimiDevSlayer)** YouTube channel.

I build web applications every day, mainly with **PHP** and **Symfony**. On the channel, I share what I actually use in real projects, as video series that build an application from A to Z.

## Why this blog?

A video is great to follow a line of reasoning, but not to find a command again or reread a piece of code. This blog turns each video into a **written course**:

- the full code, to copy instead of retyping it from the screen;
- diagrams to understand what's going on;
- common mistakes, and how to avoid them;
- exercises with solutions to check what you've learned.

All courses are **free**, in **French** and **English**.
