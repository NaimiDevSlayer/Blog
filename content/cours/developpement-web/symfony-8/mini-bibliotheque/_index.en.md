**Mini Library** is a free course based on a YouTube video series ([NAIMI Dev Slayer](https://youtube.com/@NaimiDevSlayer)) in which we build a digital library application from A to Z with **Symfony 8**:

- A public catalog with book covers
- A built-in PDF reader, to read books directly online
- A complete back office with **EasyAdmin** (books, authors, categories, users...)

Follow the chapters below in order to build the project alongside the videos.
