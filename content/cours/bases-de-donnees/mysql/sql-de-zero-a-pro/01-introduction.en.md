Welcome to this new series where we dive into the world of **SQL**, the essential language for managing and querying databases. Whether you're a beginner, want to refresh your knowledge, or just want to understand why SQL is everywhere in tech, you're in the right place.

## The program

Here's what we'll cover, chapter by chapter:

1. **What is SQL?** Why this language matters so much in modern database management, then **installing MySQL**, one of the most popular database management systems.
2. **Managing databases**: creating, deleting, renaming and switching between them.
3. **Managing tables**: creating, modifying and linking them, then **inserting, updating and deleting** data.
4. **Querying data** with `SELECT`: filtering, sorting, grouping and calculating.
5. **Advanced SQL**: **subqueries**, for more powerful queries.
6. **Advanced SQL**: **joins**, to combine data from several tables.
7. **Hands-on exercises**, in two parts, to put everything into practice.

The goal: by the end of the course, you'll walk away with **real hands-on SQL experience**.
