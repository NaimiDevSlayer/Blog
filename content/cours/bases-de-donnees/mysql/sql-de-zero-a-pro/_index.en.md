**SQL from Zero to Pro** is a free course based on a YouTube video series ([NAIMI Dev Slayer](https://youtube.com/@NaimiDevSlayer)) to learn SQL with **MySQL**, whether you're a beginner or want to refresh your knowledge:

- Create and manage databases and tables
- Link tables together (one-to-one, one-to-many, many-to-many)
- Insert, update, delete and query data
- Go further with subqueries and joins
- Practice with hands-on exercises

Follow the chapters below in order, alongside the videos.

> The videos of this course are in French. Turn on YouTube's auto-translated subtitles if needed.
